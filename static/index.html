What’s this for?

This is a Digital Footprint Self-Audit, not a people-finder. Public sources only — no login-walled scraping, no breach dumps. Instagram and Facebook cannot be reliably checked without logging in. India’s DPDP Act 2023 has a narrow public-data exception; the IT Act still applies. This is not legal advice. Rate limit: 5 live scans per hour.
